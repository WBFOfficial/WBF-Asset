## 1. Risks of Buying WBF

- **Price Volatility:** The market price of WBF may fluctuate significantly and can decline rapidly.
- **Limited Liquidity:** There may not be enough liquidity to sell WBF at the expected price or at all under certain market conditions.
- **Slippage:** Large trades relative to available liquidity may result in a substantially different execution price.
- **Smart Contract Risk:** Contract verification and renounced ownership do not eliminate all technical, economic, or security risks.
- **Market Risk:** Demand may be limited, and there is no guarantee of an active or sustainable market.
- **No Guaranteed Returns:** WBF does not guarantee profits, price appreciation, or recovery of the amount spent.

Only interact with WBF after reviewing the contract and understanding the risks. Never spend money you cannot afford to lose.

## 2. Liquidity Information

Liquidity refers to the assets available in the WBF trading pool to facilitate swaps between WBF and its paired asset.

Low liquidity may lead to:

- Significant price impact from relatively small trades.
- Higher slippage when buying or selling.
- Difficulty selling tokens at the expected price.
- Greater price volatility.
- Changes in the pool's liquidity as assets are added or removed.

Liquidity can change at any time. A displayed market price does not guarantee that a trade can be executed at that price.

**Important:** Contract verification and renounced ownership do not guarantee liquidity, market demand, or token safety.
